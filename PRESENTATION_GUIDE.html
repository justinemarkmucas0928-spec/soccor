Clinical guardrails shown in the demo

The booking screen has a deliberately conservative safety stop for potential emergency symptoms. The prototype does not diagnose patients, does not place emergency calls, and does not issue prescriptions. A production telehealth system would need clinical protocols, access control, security, auditability, privacy governance, and appropriate escalation to in-person care.
